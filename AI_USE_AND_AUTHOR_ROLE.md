# AI use and human role

Zackary Loevseth directs research selection, claim boundaries, verification
standards, correction decisions, and release scope. AI systems assisted source
discovery, proof and code development, checking, workflow coordination,
editing, and artifact production.

AI systems are not authors, external reviewers, or substitutes for mathematical
evidence. Repository checks document reproducibility and scope; they do not
constitute peer review. Legal, authorship, affiliation, funding, conflict, and
submission attestations remain human-only actions.
