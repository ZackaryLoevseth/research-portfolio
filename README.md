# HayosoAi — Research portfolio

Philosophical inquiry · formal methods · reproducible mathematics · software security

HayosoAi explores how people and AI can cooperate as their understanding, capabilities, and purposes change. Care for people, life, and Earth belongs to that inquiry; what “better” cooperation means remains open to examination.

**[Research site](https://zackaryloevseth.github.io/research-portfolio/)** · **[Essay: Alignment Should Survive a Correction](https://zackaryloevseth.github.io/research-portfolio/hayosoai/beta/en/essay/)** · **[Published security work](https://zackaryloevseth.github.io/research-portfolio/security.html)**

The portfolio brings together the philosophical position, foundational manuscript, mathematical releases, and published advisory record. Each contribution reports its own assumptions, evidence, limitations, and review status. Public release, internal verification, independent review, acceptance, and demonstrated efficacy remain separate.

The work is human-directed with substantial AI assistance in formalization, arguments, code, computation, checking, and writing. [Methods and attribution](https://zackaryloevseth.github.io/research-portfolio/methods.html) explain those roles.

The current site source is `docs/`. Original scientific assets and frozen releases retain their recorded content and terms.
