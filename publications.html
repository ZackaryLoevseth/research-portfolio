Finite groups · Graffiti3

Explicit Counterexample Families to Five Finite-Group Inequalities from Graffiti3

PUBLICATION-READY PREPRINT — EXTERNAL SPECIALIST REVIEW PENDING

Contribution

Constructs exact infinite counterexample families for conjectures C20, C21, and C22 using unitriangular groups, and for C27 and C28 using dihedral groups. The package records explicit finite witnesses including U7(2), D64, and D50.

Scope boundary

The SmallGrp audit through order 256 is database- and version-scoped corroboration, not a proof or global classification. The package does not claim absolute novelty, historical priority, or external review.

The live dedicated repository contains the curated first-party replay files. Mixed-rights private archives are excluded, and no reuse license is granted.

Semiorders · planar zonotopes

Largest-Index Zonotope Bounds for Planar Projections of Semiorder Ideals

PUBLICATION-READY PREPRINT — EXTERNAL SPECIALIST REVIEW PENDING

Contribution

Partitions nonempty ideals by their largest selected index and embeds each fiber in a translated planar zonotope, yielding exact projection bounds. Finite verification covers 318,667 cases, 3,978,057 ideals, and 2,311,018 projected points.

Scope boundary

The unrestricted parametric-closure question remains open. Finite computation corroborates but does not prove the theorem, no RR-ETI theorem bridge is claimed, bounded prior-art search does not establish novelty, and external review remains pending.

The live dedicated repository contains the curated first-party replay files. Mixed-rights private archives are excluded, and no reuse license is granted.

Graph theory · Erdős Problem #64

Excess-Degree Bounds for Minimal Counterexamples to the Erdős–Gyárfás Conjecture

PUBLICATION-READY PREPRINT — EXTERNAL SPECIALIST REVIEW PENDING

Contribution

For a hypothetical lexicographically minimal counterexample, proves the lower bound |A| ≥ 2b + s + 4 and derives associated density and edge bounds. The proof is structural and theorem-only.

Scope boundary

The Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture remains open. The work does not prove existence or nonexistence of a counterexample, cubicity, or 3-connectivity. Two-port, multipole, and finite-SAT routes are excluded from the claim; finite computation is not the proof.

The live dedicated repository contains the curated first-party replay files. The mixed-rights private archive is excluded, and no reuse license is granted.
