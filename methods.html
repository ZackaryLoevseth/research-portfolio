Methods and verification

Research that carries its audit trail

The workflow is designed to preserve mathematical scope while making computation, provenance, corrections, and release decisions inspectable. Verification supports a claim; it does not silently enlarge one.

Core principle

Proof, computation, archive integrity, prior-art review, and publication status are separate gates with separate evidence.

Verification workflow

From target selection to human-final release

A reusable control loop supports both mathematical projects and research operations.

  1. 01 · Select

    Choose a bounded target

    Define the exact question, admissible evidence, stopping conditions, and what would remain open after success.

  2. 02 · Reconstruct

    Make the argument explicit

    Separate theorem logic from discovery notes, rebuild key derivations, and record dependencies and assumptions.

  3. 03 · Compute

    Use exact checks where they fit

    Prefer integer, rational, symbolic, or exhaustive methods when the claim admits them; label numerical evidence precisely.

  4. 04 · Replay

    Run independent paths

    Pair primary scripts with independent checkers, clean-environment reproduction, manifests, and content hashes.

  5. 05 · Attack

    Audit hostile cases

    Search for boundary failures, schema conflicts, archive defects, counterexamples, and prior work before promotion.

  6. 06 · Govern

    Correct, supersede, or stop

    Preserve failed routes and valid artifacts, issue explicit corrections, stage privately, and require human-final publication approval.

Methods case

Autonomous Scientific Director workflow

METHODS CASE — VERIFIED WORKFLOW SUMMARY

A governed orchestration method coordinates problem selection, proof reconstruction, exact computation, independent replay, correction and supersession, privacy-aware staging, and human-final publication controls across several research programs.

Operational contribution

  • Maintains a claim registry that distinguishes open, conditional, finite, verified, and publication-ready states.
  • Freezes contracts and dependencies before costly runs, then records precise obstructions instead of improvising past them.
  • Preserves negative results, stopped routes, immutable certificate packets, and correction history.
  • Routes public-safe outputs separately from raw logs, internal bundles, credentials, and private staging identifiers.

Current demonstrated status

The workflow produced three canonical publication-ready packages with passing package-specific gates. That is process evidence, not external peer review, independent audit, or evidence that the method is causally superior to alternatives.

Public artifact: this public-safe methods summary and the employer-readable brief. Raw orchestration events, internal handoffs, email drafts, bundles, and private storage identifiers are intentionally excluded.
