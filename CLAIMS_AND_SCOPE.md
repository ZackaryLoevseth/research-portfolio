# Claims and scope

This repository makes portfolio-level claims about the existence, scope, and
verification state of the linked artifacts. The scientific claims are defined
by each manuscript and its accompanying claim-and-scope and verification files.

The portfolio does **not** claim:

- peer review or independent specialist approval;
- a solution of the Riemann Hypothesis;
- a solution of the global Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture;
- that finite computation proves an unrestricted theorem;
- absolute novelty or record priority;
- comparative or causal superiority of ETI/RR-ETI methods;
- a validated operational wildfire prediction system.

Public Erdős Problem #409 material is limited to its independently stated
certificate scope. Any F=104 successor requires a separate fresh replay and
release record before this site may promote it.
