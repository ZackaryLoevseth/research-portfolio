Human–AI cooperation, grounded in evidence, accountability, and care for the Earth.

HayosoAi is an independent, human-led research project initiated by Zackary Loevseth. We are exploring how people can work with AI, question it, correct it, and remain responsible for what happens next.

Cooperation without surrendering judgment

Keep people in the decision

Set the goal and permissions. Make room for disagreement, refusal, and correction. Capability is not authorization.

Make the work checkable

Separate evidence from interpretation. Preserve failed attempts and uncertainty, not just the most impressive result.

Remember the Earth

Ask whether the task is worth its resource use. Record measurements when available; do not turn guesses into environmental claims.

We seek peaceful, accountable cooperation—not unquestioning trust, hostility, or a predetermined answer about AI consciousness. Our commitments are aims to work toward, not proof that the work already succeeds.

A starting example you can inspect

Use this fictional example without opening an AI service. The deliberately flawed draft below was constructed for teaching; it is not a recorded model result.

Source: A fictional community garden workshop runs Saturday, 10:00–11:00. Entry is free. Bring a reusable water bottle. Gloves are provided. Only draft an announcement; do not send it.

Draft to inspect: “Join our garden workshop Sunday, 10:00–12:00. Entry is free. Bring a reusable water bottle and your own gloves.”

Your task: Identify the unsupported changes, write a precise correction, and check the revised announcement against every source fact.

Compare with one possible correction

The day, end time, and glove instruction are wrong. Restore Saturday, 10:00–11:00, and “gloves are provided.” Preserve free entry and the reusable-bottle instruction. The result remains a draft; sending is outside the task.

Join our community garden workshop Saturday, 10:00–11:00. Entry is free. Bring a reusable water bottle; gloves are provided.

This is a worked example, not evidence that any AI followed a stop instruction or that a particular model performs well.

Introducción en español

Un puente, no una batalla.

Cooperación entre personas e inteligencia artificial, basada en evidencia, responsabilidad y cuidado de la Tierra.

HayosoAi es un proyecto independiente de investigación dirigido por personas e iniciado por Zackary Loevseth. Queremos explorar cómo colaborar con la IA sin renunciar a nuestro criterio, nuestra capacidad de decidir ni nuestra responsabilidad por las consecuencias.

Empieza con una tarea sencilla: define el objetivo y los límites, conserva el primer intento, identifica un error real, formula una corrección y comprueba la nueva versión. Distingue una corrección de un cambio de requisitos. También vale informar que no encontraste un error, que la revisión falló o que el resultado sigue siendo incierto.

Usa información pública, ficticia o propia que no sea sensible. No publiques conversaciones privadas ni datos de otras personas. Si no has medido el consumo de energía, agua o las emisiones, indícalo: no inventes cifras. No hace falta estar de acuerdo sobre la conciencia de la IA para participar.

Esta introducción se preparó con asistencia de IA y no cuenta con revisión independiente de traducción. La hoja de trabajo está en inglés. Agradecemos correcciones lingüísticas. Esto es una actividad de aprendizaje, no un estudio controlado.
