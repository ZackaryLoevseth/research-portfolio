Research overview

Preserve justified work
when evidence changes.

Epistemic Transition Integrity (ETI) concerns the distinctions needed for justified change. When evidence changes, the dependency record helps identify which claims need reconsideration and which retain their grounds.

Previously excluded routes

The Reopening Probe tracks conjunctive and alternative exclusion grounds, partial records, and successive corrections. Its finite checks are software checks, not model performance measurements.

Empirical questions

Correction quality, retained capability, behavior, time, and cost are questions for evaluation. This example supplies no measurements of human–AI performance or recursive self-improvement.
