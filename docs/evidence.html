Evidence and corrections

Selected corrections, finite findings and unresolved verification gaps.

Source-text parity

The earlier Study 14 check could accept matching A/B identifiers and order despite changed source text. Its successor rejects the text mismatch. This establishes one tested checker property; the full empirical design remains a separate validation question.

Projection-oracle convention

The earlier unrestricted projection specification treated a strict-improvement witness as a strict hull vertex. The correction separates valid boundary witnesses from counted strict vertices. It concerns the parametric-closure engine, not the bounded semiorder theorem.

Finite counterexample

The #413 first-four-offset conjecture failed a recorded finite check. The exact counterexample and associated records remain under release review. Neither infinitude nor global held-out secrecy is established.

Prototype coverage

Earlier tests missed intended propagation and authority behavior. Their passing status did not establish complete semantic coverage; repair of every defect remains unverified.

Evidence scope

Proofs support stated claims under their assumptions; finite checkers cover exercised domains; hashes identify bytes; public releases establish availability. AI critique is an internal check. External specialist review, submission and acceptance require separate records.

Public mathematical releases contain arguments and replay materials. Private episode records, correspondence and internal ledgers remain outside this site.
