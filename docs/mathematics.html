Semiorder projection bounds

How complex can a planar projection of represented-semiorder ideals become?

For the stated inclusive positive-threshold representation, the note proves h ≤ 1 + n + inc(P), with width form h ≤ 1 + rn − r(r−1)/2 for r = min(n,w). The manuscript defines the companion vertex bound and geometric conventions. These class-specific bounds do not solve unrestricted parametric closure.

Erdős–Gyárfás excess-degree bounds

What structure would a smallest counterexample to the conjecture require?

Choose a counterexample with minimum order and then minimum size. Let A contain degree-three vertices, B the vertices of degree at least four, b = |B| and s = Σv∈B(d(v)−4). The resulting bound is |A| ≥ 2b + s + 4. Its minimality assumptions are essential.

An exact totient trajectory with F=104

Can one pointwise totient-iteration result be checked independently?

Iterate φ(n)+1 until the first prime, using the released stopping convention. The exact result F(400000287233629)=104 implies supn F(n) ≥ 104. It establishes neither a maximum nor unboundedness, a record, or a solution of Erdős #409.
