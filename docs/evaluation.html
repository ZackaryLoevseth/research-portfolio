Study 14 · prospective evaluation

Evaluation design

Comparison of correction reliability under matched evidence, including state construction and total costs. The study remains in development.

Comparison conditions

No comparative model results are established. Fixtures and readiness checks are development evidence. Comparison changes require a versioned amendment.

Development record

The successor A/B parity checker compares complete source text, identifiers and order, rejecting text changes the earlier check accepted. This demonstrates one checker correction, without establishing model-performance gains. The original 208 assertions have not been fully recovered and freshly reconstructed.

Controls remain necessary for initial-stage reliance, false completion, independent support, required reopening and valid no-change behavior. Separating construction from grading does not itself establish independent semantic labels.

Assessment requirements

Systems must construct and maintain correction distinctions without privileged answers, with full cost accounting.

  • Unit: independently constructed episode; correlated stages and assertions are not independent samples.
  • Outcomes: missed corrections, unnecessary reopening, unauthorized reliance, unsupported completion and task quality.
  • Costs: state construction and maintenance, context tokens, latency, model and tool calls, human effort.
  • Falsification: no material reliability gain under matched information and resources, or gains explained by privileged labels or uncounted construction.

Scoring, retrieval, uncertainty analysis and stopping rules must be fixed before held-out assessment. Pilot execution, new model collection and confirmatory runs remain separately governed.
