Study 14 · development stage

Evaluation with matched information.

Under what conditions does preserving premise, dependency, authority and correction state improve reliability relative to strong simpler baselines?

The four conditions remain distinct.

Controlling A/B/C/D comparison
Arm Available context Distinguishing treatment
A Full raw context No status labels or dependency-governed scheduling.
B Identical full raw context The design’s status labels, without dependency-governed scheduling.
C Competitive semantic retrieval from raw evidence Authority and dependency labels do not enter the retrieval index.
D Premise-governed scheduling Operational state must be constructed independently of hidden grading references.

No comparative model results are established. Authored fixtures and readiness checks are development evidence. Changes to comparisons require a versioned amendment.

What the development record establishes.

An earlier A/B source-parity check compared record identifiers and order, so altered source text could pass. Its successor compares the complete source text as well as identity and order and rejects that mismatch.

This is a demonstrated correction to one checker property. It does not establish improved model performance. The original set of 208 assertions was not fully recovered and freshly reconstructed; no such claim is made.

Initial-stage reliance, false completion, independent support, required reopening and genuine no-change behavior need explicit controls. Declared separation of construction and grading is not evidence of independent semantic labels.

The bridge to the formal model.

The formal model assumes correction distinctions are available. The empirical question is whether actual systems can construct and maintain them without an answer advantage, and whether any benefit survives complete cost accounting.

  • Task unit: an independently constructed episode, not each correlated stage or assertion.
  • Outcomes: missed necessary corrections, unnecessary reopening, unauthorized reliance, unsupported completion, and task quality.
  • Costs: state construction and maintenance, context tokens, latency, model and tool calls, and human effort.
  • Falsification: no material reliability improvement under matched information/resources, or a gain explained by privileged labels or uncounted construction.

Scoring, retrieval, uncertainty analysis and stopping rules must be fixed before held-out assessment. The development work does not authorize a pilot, new model collection or a confirmatory run.
