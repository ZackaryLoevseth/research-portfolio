Number theory / Computational certificate

Exact totient trajectory

Start at 400,000,287,233,629. Repeatedly apply φ(n) + 1 until the first prime. This released trajectory takes exactly 104 steps.

φ(n), Euler’s totient function, counts the positive integers up to n that are coprime to n. This explorer presents the released certificate data; a visualization is not a replacement for its verification.

Claim and boundary

Result and scope

The certificate establishes F(400000287233629) = 104 under the first-prime stopping convention. Therefore the supremum of stopping times is at least 104.

This does not establish a maximum, a record, unboundedness, a density result, or a solution to Erdős Problem #409. Different accounting conventions can shift displayed step counts; this page follows the release’s index-0 starting value and index-104 first prime.
