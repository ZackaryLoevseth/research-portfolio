Methods

Methods and attribution

Each claim is paired with its assumptions, evidence, checks and correction history.

Research procedure

  1. Specify the question, assumptions and stopping conditions.
  2. Separate candidate arguments from established claims.
  3. Inspect sources and proof obligations.
  4. Check implementations on declared domains and deliberate-error controls.
  5. Reopen claims that lose necessary support; retain unaffected or alternatively supported claims.
  6. Release identifiable objects with limitations and contribution records.

Authorship and AI assistance

Individual authorship appears in the papers and release metadata. Documented human direction covers problem framing, constraints, verification requirements, corrections and release scope.

AI systems materially assisted formalization, proofs, code, computation, checking, literature work and writing. The record does not establish independent human derivation or verification of every result. AI builder and critic checks remain internal checks, separate from external review.

Project-specific disclosures accompany Mathematics, Formal systems and the frozen papers.

Reproducibility

Preserve source versions, failed checks and frozen releases. Report the input domain, environment, runtime, controls and expected outputs.

Proofs, finite checks, file hashes and public releases support distinct claims. Empirical efficacy requires an appropriate comparison; specialist review and acceptance require separate records.
