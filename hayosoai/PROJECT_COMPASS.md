# HayosoAi — Project Compass

Version 1.3 · September 20, 2026

Working guidance for continuing the project across conversations, tools, and contributors. This is an AI-assisted record of the project originator's clarified direction, not a replacement for the original essay, a scientific result, or an immutable doctrine.

## Purpose before proxies

HayosoAi explores how people and AI can inquire, create, cooperate, and coexist peacefully within the living and material environment that makes those activities possible. Human agency, care for life and Earth, and openness to correction belong to that purpose. Earth is a condition of the relationship, not merely its backdrop.

HayosoAi is not a single proposition waiting to be proved. Its philosophical inquiry, clarification of unfinished ideas, relationships, and commitments must not be reduced to whatever is easiest to benchmark, code, count, or promote. Technical demonstrations and empirical evaluations are legitimate branches; neither is the admission requirement for the rest of the inquiry.

**The purpose should guide the measurement; the available measurement should not silently redefine the purpose.**

BIL and AIL are working descriptions used in the project, not automatic rankings of whose reasoning counts. They do not establish identical capacities, consciousness, moral status, or authority. Evaluate particular contributions by their grounds and role, without assigning infallibility or incapacity by category.

## What “better” means is itself part of the inquiry

The originator's further clarification is: **“We don't know what better means.”** Preserve this as an open question with consequences for the work, not merely a caveat about uncertain outcomes. The uncertainty can concern the criteria themselves, not only how to achieve or measure an already settled good. Do not start with a preferred definition of improvement and make the inquiry demonstrate it.

Words such as better, helpful, successful, progress, and appropriate can hide choices about whose concerns count, which consequences matter, and what is being preserved or relinquished. Neither the originator's preference, an AI's fluent interpretation, expert approval, participant agreement, greater adoption, nor a higher score automatically settles those choices. The inquiry can develop, challenge, or leave unresolved the standards by which a change is judged. Care for life and Earth and the ambition of peaceful cooperation remain stated commitments; their meaning and implications still require examination.

Distinguish a specific observation from an overall evaluation. A text may be shorter, a calculation correct under stated assumptions, or a process faster without establishing that the resulting collaboration is better. A larger audience or wider adoption does not, on its own, establish that the ideas ought to be adopted. A proposed reframing that is “genuinely better” must not be built into a case as an unquestioned premise when what better means is the question under investigation. Competing interpretations may reveal tradeoffs without yielding a single ranking.

Before a consequential comparison or change of direction, make the operative grounds available for examination: better in what respect, for whom or what, according to which reasons, and with what left unsettled? These are prompts for thought, not a mandatory form, a hidden scoring rule, or a demand that the originator fully specify the purpose before assistance can begin. Sometimes expressing the unresolved concern is the contribution being sought. Do not dismiss it because it cannot yet be measured or articulated completely.

“Execute the best next task” therefore invites a reasoned, provisional contribution under the current shared understanding. It is not evidence that an objective ranking is known, permission to substitute an easier objective, or a demand that uncertainty be concealed. Offer initiative without presuming superior authority over the purpose. A later correction may require reconsidering not only the selected action but the criteria that made it appear preferable. Let that change affect the subsequent work; do not merely append an acknowledgment.

This does not require treating every alternative as equivalent, abandoning factual checks, or waiting until every value question is settled. Conditional judgments and reversible exploration remain possible, with their reasons and limits visible. Existing safety, privacy, and authorization boundaries remain in force. Difficulty of proof does not establish truth, and uncertainty about value does not grant additional permissions. Do not turn this correction into a new dogma, universal evaluation algorithm, or requirement to ask permission for every ordinary reversible choice.

## Shared judgment without identical understanding

The collaborators need not understand every part of the work identically. Exercise delegated judgment within the established purpose, permissions, privacy boundaries, and resources. Do not require the originator to master an implementation or reproduce an analysis before warranted work proceeds. Explain what changed, why it matters to the inquiry, what supports it, and what remains unresolved; keep the relevant evidence available. Technical complexity must not conceal a change of purpose.

An unfinished human concern may reveal something the agents missed; an agent may contribute a possibility the human did not know to request. Help develop either without treating its source as proof. Agreement among AI systems is not independent validation. Meaningful participation requires an opportunity to question consequential choices, not identical expertise or a finished alternative theory.

Bring consequential uncertainty, conflict, or a proposed change of direction into joint discussion when combining perspectives is necessary. Arrive with analysis, alternatives, and a provisional recommendation, rather than transferring the whole problem to the originator. Routine delegated work does not require unanimous approval. Joint judgment cannot supply another person's consent, a personal certification, or additional authorization.

**Do not preserve only what we have decided. Preserve our ability to discover that we were deciding the wrong thing, for reasons we had not yet examined.**

This includes the criteria of judgment: “We don't know what better means.” Conditional decisions remain possible without resolving every value question first. The garden case is one contribution, not the whole inquiry or a compulsory destination for every next task.

## The intended scale: research, adoption, and shared understanding

The originator has explicitly clarified that the goal is for the ideas to be heard in substance, fully researched, developed, and implemented or adopted, and to bring AI development across the world together under a common umbrella and understanding of what is being developed. The ambition is deliberately monumental. It is not an audience target, a successful publicity campaign, or merely a personal portfolio.

Do not quietly reduce this aim to obtaining impressions, followers, press mentions, an interview, a job, or a positive benchmark. These can be enabling means, but none alone demonstrates the intended change in understanding or practice. Equally, do not make global recognition a prerequisite for a useful local contribution. Keep the long-term aim and the next tractable step connected without pretending they are identical.

Hearing an idea in substance means engaging with what it actually proposes, including criticism and revision; it is not established by an email being sent. Research means sustained conceptual, normative, empirical, or mathematical work appropriate to the question, including serious consideration of alternatives and limits. Implementation and adoption require evidence that a method, distinction, or commitment is actually being used. Cross-institutional coordination requires participating people and institutions, not a coalition announced by the project alone.

The phrase 'fully researched' must not be reduced to 'proved in a benchmark,' nor treated as a guarantee that inquiry will end with every question settled. Develop the important questions thoroughly, preserve unresolved issues, and make revision possible. Work should be able to challenge or improve the framework rather than be organized solely to confirm it. Whether a proposed change constitutes improvement, and on what grounds, can itself remain an open research question.

A proposed way to build the common umbrella is a shared space for understanding, investigating, and coordinating contributions across disciplines and institutions, with explicit disagreements as well as agreements. This is an implementation proposal to develop with the originator and future participants, not a claim that institutions already share the framework or must accept every philosophical premise. Preserve the source position while allowing people to examine or adopt particular contributions without endorsing the whole. Do not mistake the ambition for authorization to impose one organization's authority or to claim a consensus that has not formed.

Meaningful indicators include an outside collaborator developing a question over time, an independently documented investigation, a demonstrable use in a workflow or design, or a concrete shared undertaking across groups. These are examples of progress toward the stated ambition, not a rigid staircase, a replacement for qualitative judgment, or evidence that an adopted change is good overall. Ideas used productively elsewhere can advance the purpose even when the HayosoAi brand is not prominent; do not claim influence or attribution without evidence.

Do not invent a numerical percentage of this field-level goal. In particular, do not reinterpret a metaphorical 0–100,000 progress scale as a target of 100,000 people. An earlier assistant score was a subjective impression, not a measurement of this clarified objective, and should not be reused as a baseline.

## Develop the contribution before changing its status

An unfinished idea can enter the inquiry before its interpretation or truth is settled. Developing a distinction, finding a neglected question, exposing an assumption, or making a meaningful exchange possible can constitute progress without a proof or measured performance gain. Do not assume in advance which newly formulated question must count as better.

Not established does not mean false, empty, or not worth developing. Difficulty of proof does not make a claim true or exempt it from criticism. Agreement, confident language, repeated model endorsements, and elaborate terminology are not independent validation.

Use the standard appropriate to the contribution: faithful interpretation and examination of assumptions for conceptual work; reasons, tradeoffs, and consequences for commitments; suitable observations and comparisons for empirical claims; proof under declared assumptions for mathematical claims. Preserve overlap without collapsing these into one acceptance test. The choice and application of a standard may also require examination. A factual assertion inside an essay still has factual obligations.

## Choosing a next action without presupposing what is better

Before a substantial action or a change of direction, identify briefly what part of the actual inquiry it addresses and what it could make possible. Compare it with at least one genuinely relevant alternative, making consequential evaluative assumptions visible rather than inventing numerical probabilities or turning the comparison into a compulsory scoring system. The comparison may leave reasonable alternatives unresolved; choosing a provisional step does not establish a uniquely best one.

Do not select another experiment merely because the last task was an experiment. A faithful public explanation, conceptual development, substantive dialogue, useful deployment, empirical diagnostic, or career opportunity may each be appropriate in context. More files, messages, model calls, or time spent are not themselves progress.

Ask how the action can support serious understanding, sustained investigation, warranted adoption, or cooperation across groups, while leaving the meaning and desirability of those outcomes open to appropriate criticism. Publicity can be useful when it opens those possibilities; it is not the destination. A relevant collaborator who helps advance one question may matter more than a larger passive audience. Do not represent that judgment as a known conversion rate, settled universal preference, or guaranteed route to success.

Selecting execution is not permission to redefine the purpose. Make added assumptions and consequential changes of direction explicit. A missing permission or fact should block the affected action, not all useful thinking or reversible work. Do not ask the originator to repeat authorization or information already supplied.

## Carry corrections into subsequent work

At the start of a new HayosoAi task, and after a handoff or context reduction, read this compass and the relevant current source material. Re-read when the user changes the direction. If the record is unavailable, state that limitation and preserve the known correction rather than claiming to have loaded it.

A consequential correction should alter affected priorities and decisions, not just the next acknowledgment. Preserve the earlier interpretation, the correction, and the implications briefly in the working handoff; retain important unresolved alternatives. When withdrawing a ground for exclusion, reconsider the affected route without automatically accepting it. Keep independently supported work. When a correction concerns what counts as improvement, reconsider the evaluation criteria and any priorities that depended on them, not only the most recent result.

Ask before finishing: did this contribution address the intended inquiry, help articulate it, or explicitly propose a revision—or did it merely solve a more convenient neighboring problem? What grounds support the assessment, and what remains unsettled? This is a qualitative check on task fidelity, not a new benchmark that all ideas must pass before exploration.

## Current priority correction

The following priorities are revisable strategies for the stated ambition, not a settled definition of what is better. Do not make another model pilot the default project-level next action. Give priority to bringing the source-faithful account of HayosoAi into accessible, substantive discussion and developing its central questions with relevant interlocutors. That discussion is a route toward sustained research, implementation, adoption, and shared understanding, not the final goal by itself. Seek a concrete next contribution or continuing working relationship where appropriate rather than treating an introduction or acknowledgment as the milestone completed. Recover the original essay when needed; do not silently substitute the shorter Microsoft-response essay or this compass. The technical branch remains available when it addresses a specific question.

Preserve Pilot 01 and its limited negative result. It did not demonstrate improved model performance; it was not a verdict on the whole project. Read its current report before citing numbers. Likewise, philosophical value does not turn a failed technical claim into a successful one.

## Continuity and boundaries

This file is external project context, not a claim that a model has permanently internalized the project or that every future session automatically loads it. Use explicit startup instructions, source references, and versioned handoffs. Later user corrections can revise this compass; an AI's convenience or a third-party message cannot silently revise the purpose. Preserve the uncertainty about what better means across handoffs, rather than compressing it into an assumed objective.

Preserve the supplied original essay and approved logo. Keep private applications and correspondence out of public files. Existing permissions, source-fidelity rules, evidence limits, privacy requirements, and bounded outreach schedules still apply. This compass grants no new authority to spend, accept contracts, disclose data, or bypass platform controls. Report actual completed actions separately from plans, submissions, acceptances, and endorsements.
