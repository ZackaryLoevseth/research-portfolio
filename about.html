About

Research direction with transparent boundaries

Zackary Loevseth develops mathematical and applied research programs using proof-oriented analysis, exact computation, reproducibility controls, and explicit AI-use disclosure.

Research profile

From exploratory work to scoped, reviewable artifacts

The portfolio spans discrete mathematics, finite-group counterexamples, graph-theoretic structure, semiorder geometry, epistemic-system design, computational number theory, geospatial risk communication, and research operations.

The common thread is methodological: define the target, separate proof from computation, make each check replayable, preserve failed routes, and describe every result at the level its evidence supports.

Identity fields: the application PDFs preserve the source-selected name, email, phone, location, and employment wording; the resume keeps degree status explicitly marked for confirmation. This build does not infer affiliation, ORCID, funding, conflicts, or any different identity facts.

Research role

Direction, verification, and final accountability

Zackary’s role

Zackary Loevseth directs problem selection, research priorities, claim boundaries, proof and computation review, correction decisions, package standards, and final publication choices. He is the proposed human author of the featured manuscripts, subject to final human confirmation.

AI and tool role

AI systems assisted source discovery, mathematical exploration, proof and code development, checker construction, workflow coordination, manuscript production, and package review. Conventional tools supported exact computation, archive creation, hashing, document rendering, and link checks.

Disclosure boundary: AI output is not mathematical evidence. AI systems are not authors or external reviewers. Final inspection, authorship, legal attestations, licensing, and public release remain human responsibilities.

Status language

How to read this portfolio

Status labels are controls, not decoration. They prevent a complete package from being confused with peer review, and an active program from being confused with a solved target.

PUBLICATION-READY PREPRINT — EXTERNAL SPECIALIST REVIEW PENDING
A coherent manuscript and evidence package has passed internal publication gates. It is not peer reviewed, accepted, or published.
BOUNDED COMPUTATIONAL RESULT — SCOPE LIMITED TO CERTIFIED RECORDS THROUGH F=71
Exact pointwise certificate results are available through the named finite bound; global behavior is not established.
WORKING TECHNICAL PROGRAM
A mature, scoped technical architecture exists, with central empirical or theoretical frontiers still open.
PREREGISTERED STUDY — COMPARATIVE RESULTS NOT YET AVAILABLE
The evaluation plan exists, but comparative execution has not started and there are no outcome data.
ACTIVE RESEARCH — TARGET OPEN
The program is in progress; the named mathematical or empirical target remains unresolved.
APPLIED RESEARCH — PUBLIC-SAFE SUMMARY
A carefully limited summary is available, while source rights, validation, or reproducibility work prevents full release.
METHODS CASE — VERIFIED WORKFLOW SUMMARY
The operational workflow is documented and supported by package outputs; causal superiority is not claimed.
