Methods and verification

Evidence is part of the research result

Claims move through a reproducible workflow: scope definition, proof reconstruction, exact computation where appropriate, independent replay, hostile audit, correction or supersession, and human-final release controls.

01

Scope before promotion

Finite, conditional, internal, and process evidence are labeled before public claims are drafted.

02

Replayable verification

Packages pair exact checks with manifests, hashes, source instructions, and clean reproduction steps.

03

Human-final control

AI assists research and production, but AI output is not evidence, authorship, or external review.
